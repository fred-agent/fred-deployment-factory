# Privacy Notice - Fredlab Playground

Last updated: June 2026

Fredlab Playground processes the information required for authentication, authorization, service operation, and security audit.

Technical and usage data may include login identity, groups, roles, application logs, session metadata, and actions performed in the platform.

Users should avoid entering or uploading unnecessary personal data. Any ingestion of personal data must comply with the project framework, applicable internal rules, and data protection obligations.

For questions about data processed in this environment, contact the Fredlab administrators.
